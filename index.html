The initiative, in brief

Eighteen months to build the pipeline

Tap

Multifamily buildings

10–15

Apartment buildings made retrofit-ready by month 6: energy assessed, scope written, financing identified

First gate at month 6. Planning in months 1 to 6, testing for the remaining 12. Savings must pass to renters.
Tap

Existing homes

75–100

Owner-occupied houses grouped into one retrofit program by month 12, with affordable financing in place

Gate at month 12. Twelve months to build the structure, six to test it. A family refinances at their current income and comes out the same or better.
Tap

Commercial properties

10

Storefronts and commercial sites assessed by month 12: 7 to 8 retrofits plus 2 to 3 new developments

Seven to eight retrofits and two to three new developments, connected to planned projects like Englewood Connect. Gate at month 12.
Tap

New-home lots

160

City-owned vacant lots made shovel-ready for new net-zero houses: plans, costs, financing and developers lined up by month 18

About 20% of 800 city-owned vacant lots. Developers, housing type, cost, capital stack and early buyers identified before month 18.

The spine

Capital stacks and term sheets. The capital exists; the pipeline does not. We build the pipeline so money moves into Englewood, and keeps moving.

The partners

Chicago Urban League on training, placement and retrofits. East Lake Management on the 160 lots. The Consortium on program design and capital. BLR Center holds the award.

The promise

No displacement. Built for the community, with the community. Affordable in 2026 and still affordable in 2050.

Our message

Not "can they afford to move in."
Can they afford to stay?

Tap

Move in

Pathways to ownership

A loan nobody else can offer: appraisal gap closed by subsidy, credit enhancements for the family with a foreclosure or old medical bills, and a $400,000 home that stays worth $400,000.
Tap

Stay

Lower bills for life

Englewood households spend 15 to 26 percent of income on utilities; 6 percent is the line for energy burden. Insulation, windows, HVAC and efficient appliances bring the bill down and keep it down.
Tap

Pass it down

Generational wealth

Home ownership is where most American families hold their wealth. Jobs come out of the same pipeline: the work creates demand, training and placement fill it from the community.
